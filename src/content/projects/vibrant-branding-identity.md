## 1. Project Background & Challenge

Aura Coffee is a premium, single-origin coffee roastery targeting urban millennials. They needed a visual identity that stood out from the sea of minimalist, black-and-white coffee brands. The goal was to build a brand identity that felt organic, premium, and highly energetic, reflecting the vibrant source regions (Ethiopia, Colombia, Sumatra) of their coffee beans.

---

## 2. Creative Concept & Typography

We crafted a custom typographic mark and a bold, fluid illustration style to represent the complex flavors and profiles of the coffee.

### Color Palette

- **Ethiopia Bloom**: Warm coral (`#FF6F61`) and sunflower yellow (`#FFD166`).
- **Colombia Nectar**: Lush turquoise (`#06D6A0`) and deep teal (`#073B4C`).
- **Brand Core**: Cream white (`#FCF8F2`) and espresso brown (`#2E1A11`).

### Packaging System Design

The packaging wraps featured distinct abstract illustrations representing notes of fruit, chocolate, and floral elements:

1.  **Floral Roasts**: Swirling pastel gradients.
2.  **Nutty / Chocolate Roasts**: Earthy, geometric shapes.
3.  **Experimental Micro-lots**: Holographic highlights and high-contrast typography.
