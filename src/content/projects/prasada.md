## 1. Project Background & Challenge

Prasada Fusion is a culinary catering brand offering a diverse blend of fusion meals, rice bowls, and premium western-oriental dishes like Beef Stroganoff, Roasted Chicken, and Beef Black Pepper. To grow their online presence and drive daily catering orders, they needed an appetizing, dynamic social media branding system.

The primary design challenge was to create versatile social media templates that make food photography look irresistibly fresh and high-end while keeping weekly menu recommendations visually distinct, engaging, and easy to update for social media feeds and stories.

---

## 2. Creative Concept & Visual Strategy

We developed a bold visual framework utilizing rich background textures, vibrant color accents, and expressive typography to highlight the texture, flavor, and quality of each dish.

### Color Palette

- **Charcoal Slate**: Dark Textured Gray (`#191919`) — provides high contrast to make food photography pop.
- **Flame Crimson**: Rich Maroon Red (`#8B2628`) — evokes warmth, appetite, and culinary passion.
- **Sky Azure**: Vibrant Cyan/Blue (`#00A3E0`) — adds freshness and energetic contrast for daytime rice bowl promos.
- **Saffron Yellow**: Mustard/Gold Accent (`#E0A800`) — highlights recommendations, prices, and CTA buttons.
- **Crisp White**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) — for clean, readable headers and brush-style script fonts.

### Social Media & Content System Design

The visual content was organized into distinct weekly thematic templates:

1. **Saturday Recommendation Series**:
   Eye-catching weekly feature layouts using bold brush typography, flame graphics, and vibrant background contrasts (Azure Blue, Crimson Red, and Charcoal) to highlight featured dishes.
2. **Signature Menu Highlights**:
   Dark-themed, elegant layouts featuring golden paint-stroke textures, fresh herb accents, and high-contrast photography for premium menu items like *Beef Stroganoff* and *Beef Black Pepper*.
3. **Rice Bowl & Quick Meal Promos**:
   Bright, energetic sky-blue themes showcasing portable rice bowls and everyday comfort foods targeting office workers and students.
4. **Call-to-Action (CTA) Integration**:
   Standardized social media footer bars featuring WhatsApp order contacts and Instagram handles (`@prasada.fusion`) to convert viewers into catering customers seamlessly.
