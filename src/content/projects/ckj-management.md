## 1. Project Background & Challenge

CKJ Management is a professional training organization dedicated to mentoring school-based Paskibra (Honor Guard) and Scout (Pramuka) extracurricular programs. Their core mission is to cultivate discipline, leadership skills, and championship mindsets, guiding youth teams toward top achievements in regional and national competitions.

The primary challenge was to establish a cohesive brand identity that balances **discipline, authority, and achievement** with a **modern, high-energy aesthetic** that resonates with middle and high school students. The social media visual strategy needed to build institutional pride, showcase competition victories as strong social proof, and attract new school partnerships.

---

## 2. Creative Concept & Visual Direction

The visual direction draws inspiration from the precision of march drills and the camaraderie of scouting culture. We combined sharp geometric angles with dynamic layouts—reminiscent of professional sports media—to evoke a champion-level presence.

### Color Palette

- **Victory Gold**: Celebratory Gold (`#FFC72C`) — representing excellence, trophies, and top achievements.
- **Valor Navy**: Deep Navy (`#0A192F`) — symbolizing discipline, professionalism, and structure.
- **Energetic Crimson**: Passionate Red (`#E63946`) — embodying courage, youth energy, and determination.
- **Base Neutrals**: Clean White (`#F8F9FA`) and Carbon Black (`#121212`) for high-contrast typography and readability.

### Social Media & Branding System Design

The social media design framework was structured into distinct categories tailored for strategic audience engagement:

1. **Championship & Achievement Highlights**:
   High-contrast layouts featuring bold typography, dramatic photo lighting, and custom medal/trophy vector accents to highlight competition victories.
2. **Training & Discipline Showcase**:
   Desaturated or monochrome photography paired with bold gold/red accents to emphasize focus, physical rigor, and teamwork during training sessions.
3. **Leadership & Drill Tips (Educational Content)**:
   Clean, infographic-style carousel templates delivering practical drill guides, scout knots, and leadership takeaways in digestible formats.
4. **Recruitment & Event Collateral**:
   High-impact promotional posters and banners designed for physical prints and digital campaigns to drive enrollment for training camps and seminars.
