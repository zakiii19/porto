## 1. Project Background & Overview

Indonesia possesses immense potential for natural water streams, but harnessing them for electricity is often hindered by the lack of efficient, centralized tracking systems. This UI/UX project began as a collaborative research project with my university lecturer to digitalize and monitor **Micro-Hydro Power Plants (PLTMH)**.

PLTMH represents a critical renewable energy resource in rural and remote regions of Indonesia. By focusing on real-time energy output data and device health, this monitoring interface ensures optimal generator operations and allows swift identification of performance drops or hardware failures.

---

## 2. Project Goals

1.  **Enhance Efficiency**: Enable operators to monitor current PLTMH electricity outputs in real-time to increase operational efficiency.
2.  **Streamline Troubleshooting**: Empower technicians to track active device conditions, making it simple to identify failures or drop-offs immediately.
3.  **Optimize Energy Availability**: Support administrators with data-backed, timely decision-making to maintain stable power grids.

---

## 3. Empathize & Define

To map the requirements of a specialized industrial interface, we analyzed three primary user roles:
-   **Operators**: Need reassurance that the generators are running safely overnight.
-   **Technicians**: Need precise data regarding which component is failing when power outputs drop, removing guess-work.
-   **Operations Managers**: Need solid, aggregated numbers for reports without manually calculating variables from paper logbooks.

### User Persona
We formulated the persona of **Andi Cahyono (28)**, an operator at a rural micro-hydro plant in Desa Sungai Indah, West Kalimantan. Andi needs:
-   Instant, real-time energy metrics.
-   Immediate alarm alerts for system failures.
-   Extreme legibility under diverse lighting environments.
-   Simple access to historical data logs.

---

## 4. Ideation: "Crazy 8" & Solution Mapping

Using the **Crazy 8** sketching technique, we rapidly brainstormed mobile layout concepts, focusing specifically on displaying data graphs clearly on compact mobile screens.

### Design Solutions
-   **Real-Time Data Visualization**: Clear, high-contrast, real-time charts showing turbine speeds (RPM) and electrical output (Watts, Volts, Amps) across daily, weekly, or monthly filter views.
-   **Alert Customization**: A dedicated notification tab showing automated system warnings (e.g., *\"Turbine rotating too slowly\"*).
-   **Thumb-Friendly Statistics**: High-priority metrics and quick navigation controls placed in comfortable touch zones for field usability.

---

## 5. Visual Direction & Final Mockups

-   **Color Palette**: Dominated by shades of tech-blue, conveying clean, eco-friendly energy and technological precision.
-   **Typography**: Highly legible, bold sans-serif typefaces designed to be readable in high-glare, outdoor, and low-light environments.
-   **Data Tables**: Clean layouts with contrasting key indicators (e.g., showing green checkmarks for healthy status and warnings for failures).
