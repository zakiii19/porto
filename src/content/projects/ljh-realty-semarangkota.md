## 1. Project Background & Challenge

LJH Realty Semarang Kota is a premier real estate agency specializing in buying, selling, and renting residential and commercial properties across Indonesia. Operating in a competitive market, they needed a cohesive digital design system to elevate their property listings, establish strong regional brand authority, and connect effectively with homebuyers and investors.

The primary challenge was to transform complex property specs—pricing, location advantages, floor plans, and investment yields—into visually striking, easy-to-digest social media assets while maintaining a professional, trustworthy, and high-end brand aesthetic.

---

## 2. Design Strategy & Visual Identity

We developed a structured design system focused on clarity, prestige, and high conversion, taking direct inspiration from the brand's iconic logo color scheme to ensure visual consistency across all digital channels.

### Color Palette

- **Vibrant Red**: Bright Red (`#E52421`) — drives call-to-action (CTA) visibility, highlights urgent deals, and commands immediate focus.
- **Warm Amber**: Bright Orange (`#F7931E`) — adds energy, enthusiasm, and warmth to marketing banners.
- **Sunburst Yellow**: Golden Yellow (`#FFCC00`) — draws attention to special promos, price drops, and featured listing badges.
- **Core Black**: Solid Black (`#000000`) — grounds the visual identity with authority, sophistication, and modern contrast.
- **Crisp White**: Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) — maintains readability and clean, modern typography for complex listing details.

### Social Media & Marketing System Design

The visual content layout was categorized into targeted strategic templates:

1. **Property Feature & Listing Showcases**:
   Clean, modern templates designed to showcase high-resolution architectural photography, key features (bedrooms, land size, certificate status), and price points with clear CTA buttons.
2. **Corporate Milestones & Campaigns**:
   Special anniversary and achievement post designs celebrating brand legacy (such as the 13th Anniversary campaign), building long-term trust with buyers and property owners.
3. **Educational & Market Insights**:
   Infographic carousels breaking down property investment tips, mortgage guides (KPR), and Semarang urban development updates to nurture target audiences.
4. **Agent Recognition & Testimonials**:
   Professional spotlight templates highlighting top-performing agents, client success stories, and closed-deal announcements to reinforce market credibility.
